Tabouleh

    ▢ ½ cup quinoa
    ▢ ¾ cup water
    ▢ pomegranate
    ▢ ⅓ cup extra virgin olive oil
    ▢ ½ teaspoon salt
    ▢ ½ teaspoon black pepper
    ▢ ⅓ cup lemon juice
    ▢ 3 bunches parsley finely chopped (3 cups)
    ▢ ¼ cup fresh mint finely chopped
    ▢ 4 green onions very finely chopped
    ▢ 2 tomatoes finely chopped

Instructions

    Heat a small saucepan over medium-high heat. Add the quinoa to a dry pan and toast it, stirring frequently until it begins to pop, turns somewhat golden brown, and smells nutty, about 5 minutes.
    Add water and bring it to a boil. Reduce to a simmer and cover. Cook until the quinoa is tender, about 15 minutes. Turn off the heat and let the quinoa rest for 5 minutes. Uncover, fluff with a fork, and let it cool to room temperature.
    While the quinoa cools, make the dressing by stirring together olive oil, lemon juice, salt, and pepper in a bowl.
    In a large bowl, place the chopped parsley, chopped mint leaves, green onions, and tomatoes. Add the cooled quinoa and pour the dressing over the top. Stir gently to combine. Taste and add more salt and pepper if you like. Serve at room temperature or chilled.    ▢ ½ cup quinoa

Cripsy Chilli Beef

Beef
1 egg
1-2 onions
1 Red pepper thinly sliced
1 tsp Garlic puree
1 tsp Ginger puree
a lot Sweet chilli
1 tbsp Tomato puree
1/2 tbsp Ketchup
1 tbsp Rice vinegar
1 tbsp Honey
Little bit of lemon juice

1 tbsp Soy sauce?

Spices:
Pinch of salt and pepper
Dash of Msg
1 tbsp Brown sugar
2 tbsp? potato starch
Chinese 5 spice
Chilli flakes

1.	Thinly slice the beef
2.	Mix in one egg and some potato starch
3.	In a separate bowl, add potato starch, salt, dash of chinese 5 spice, black and white pepper to a bowl
4.	Dip the beef in the dry mixture and then into the hot oil
5.	Cook for 1.5 minutes until crispy and golden brown

chilli paneer recipe
ingredients
	◦	chilli flakes/powder
	◦	red or green chilli
	◦	green spring onions
	◦	ketchup
	◦	dark soy sauce
	◦	rice vinegar
	◦	green chilli sauce
	◦	tomato puree
	✓	sesame oil
	✓	corn flour
	✓	plain flour
	✓	paneer
	✓	2/3 garlic cloves
	✓	pepper
	✓	2/3 onions

steps
	1.	add 3 tablespoons of plain flour and 3 tablespoons of corn flour, plus chilli flakes & salt to a bowl.
	2.	then add about 8/9 tablespoons of water to this mixture
	3.	cut paneer into cubed sized pieces and place this into the bowl. mix so the paneer is all coated
	4.	heat a pan with olive oil and fry the paneer until golden brown on all sides. make sure they’re not touching or they’ll stick together
	5.	drain them on kitchen tissue or in a colander, and put to one side
	6.	then cut up your vegetables (you could do this earlier if you prefer) into small cubes (peppers and onions). also cut your chilli, spring onions and garlic
	7.	heat a pan with a tiny bit of olive oil and saute your garlic
	8.	switch flame to high, add sesame oil and then fry all other vegetables
	9.	once they’ve cooked almost all the way (they can be crispy or soft depending on your preference), add:
	11.		11.	ketchup*, 3 tablespoons green chilli sauce, 2 table spoons soy sauce, 1 1/2 of vinegar to the pan. mix together.
	12.	add your paneer to the pan and mix everything together
	13.	season with chilli flakes, basil, salt, little bit of veggie stock cube, cumin, black pepper (whatever really)
	14.	add more ketchup/sauce if needed
	15.	serve with rice/naan/top w sesame seeds etc :)

*i don’t measure ketchup, just add however much you need. i don’t really measure the other sauces now either but it’s helpful for the first time to make sure you don’t make it too spicy

Chicken taco recipe
	◦	7 chicken thighs (whole pack, 1kg)
	◦	Garlic and ginger paste
	◦	Tortilla wrap
	◦	Coriander
	◦	Cheddar cheese
	◦	Cumin powder
	◦	Cajun seasoning or all purpose seasoning or both
	◦	Paprika
	◦	White pepper
	◦	Black pepper
	◦	Mixed herbs
	◦	Chilli flakes
	◦	Honey
	◦	BBQ sauce
	◦	1/2 Lime or 1/2 lemon
	◦	1 chicken stock cube
	◦	Tomato puree
	◦	1 red onion
	◦	Olive oil

	1.	To begin with you want to marinate your chicken add the cumin powder, all purpose seasoning, Cajun seasoning, black pepper, mixed herbs, paprika, white pepper, olive oil, barbecue sauce, juice of half a 🍋‍🟩 and tomato purée to a bowl make these together and then add your chicken thighs.
	2.	Next, you want to thinly slice your onion along with the grating your ginger and garlic (or if u have a paste alr u obvs don’t need to grate these).
	3.	Now add a drizzle of olive oil to a pot and add your marinated chicken thighs, you just want to sear both sides - I ended up searing for about five minutes on each side and then remove these from the pot.
	4.	In the same pot, you want to add your onions, garlic and ginger and cook these together.
	5.	Add the chicken back into the pot and add your chicken stock - for my chicken stock I used one chicken oxo cube that has been dissolved in a little bit of boiling water, and then fill up the pot with some more water.
	6.	The water level should almost submerge the chicken thighs. now add the lid onto the pot and cook this down for about 30mins-1hr (depending on how much chicken u have)
	7.	Take off the lid, remove and shred the chicken thighs and add back to the pot.
	8.	With the lid off now, cook the chicken until most of the liquid has evaporated.
	9.	I usually add a little bit more BBQ sauce to the chicken at this stage and stir it in.
	10.	Dip the tortilla wrap in the sauce, add to a pan and then add the chicken, cheese and coriander.
	11.	Serve😊

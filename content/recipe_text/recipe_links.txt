BBQ chicken and sausage pasta recipe
	◦	Chicken
	◦	Frankfurter
	◦	1 tomato
	◦	1/4 of a red pepper
	◦	1 onion
	◦	1tbsp tomato puree
	◦	1 tsp garlic puree
	◦	1/2 chicken stock cube
	◦	Pasta
	◦	BBQ sauce
	◦	Cheese (optional)

Instructions
	1.	Salt and boil pasta for 10 minutes, saving the pasta water.
	2.	If you’re using raw chicken, season and cook this first then remove from the pot.
	3.	Finely chop 1 tomato, 1/4 of a pepper and an onion - add this to a pot alongside the tomato puree.
	4.	Cook down for at least 10 minutes.
	5.	Move to the side then cook the garlic paste for 30 seconds and then mix in.
	6.	Move this sauce to the side and cook your chopped frankfurter for 3-4 minutes.
	7.	Dissolve 1/2 a chicken stock cube in the pasta water and add this to the pot.
	8.	Cook down until the sauce is the consistency of your choice.
	9.	Add in the BBQ sauce and mix.
	10.	Add in the chicken and pasta.
	11.	Top with cheese (optional).

Protein apple crumble
Ingredients
	◦	1 apple (I used braeburn)
	◦	Chia seeds
	◦	Flax seeds
	◦	Cinnamon
	◦	Oats
	◦	Water or milk
	◦	1/2 shortbread
	◦	Sugar or other sweetener (optional)

Method
	1.	cut up the apple into small cubes
	2.	To a pot add the apple, chia seeds, flax seeds and a bit of water - then cook for 5-10 minutes (if the water evaporates be sure to add more)
	3.	Add your oats and cook like normal
	4.	I like to add 2 tsp of golden sugar to mine
	5.	Crumble 1/2 a shortbread on top

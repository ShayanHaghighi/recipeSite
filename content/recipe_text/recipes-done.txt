Protein apple crumble
Ingredients
	◦	1 apple (I used braeburn)
	◦	Chia seeds
	◦	Flax seeds
	◦	Cinnamon
	◦	Oats
	◦	Water or milk
	◦	1/2 shortbread
	◦	Sugar or other sweetener (optional)

Method
	1.	cut up the apple into small cubes
	2.	To a pot add the apple, chia seeds, flax seeds and a bit of water - then cook for 5-10 minutes (if the water evaporates be sure to add more)
	3.	Add your oats and cook like normal
	4.	I like to add 2 tsp of golden sugar to mine
	5.	Crumble 1/2 a shortbread on top

Shepherds pie
	◦	500g minced beef
	◦	2 onions
	◦	1 carrot
	◦	Balsamic vinegar
	◦	Old bay seasoning
	◦	Salt
	◦	Pepper
	◦	2 Tomatoes
	◦	Garlic
	◦	Tomato puree
	◦	Rosemary
	◦	Thyme
	◦	7 medium sided potatoes (or 5 large)
	◦	Cheddar cheese
	◦	Bay leaf
	◦	Ketchup

Method
	1.	Peel, cut and boil 7 medium sized potatoes until soft.
	2.	Whilst this is boiling cut your carrot, onions and tomatoes into small cubes.
	3.	Add some oil to a pot and add in the carrot, onions and tomatoes.
	4.	To this pot add in a tsp of garlic and some tomato puree.
	5.	Add in the minced beef and season with the seasonings mentioned and add in a splash of balsamic vinegar.
	6.	Cook the beef until cooked through (6–10minutes)
	7.	Once the potatoes are finished, mash season with salt, pepper, mixed herbs, cheese etc and mash them (you may also choose to add butter and/or milk to your mash)
	8.	In your baking dish first add a thin layer of cheese at the bottom (optional)
	9.	Then add your beef layer.
	10.	Then another thin layer of cheese.
	11.	Then spread the mash potato onto this.
	12.	Finally top with cheese.
	13.	Bake in the oven at 200 fan oven (220 otherwise/gas mark 6) for 20-25mins or until the cheese is golden on top.
